About Edward Pena de Dios

Academic History and Goals

My interest in technology has been a driving force behind my decision to pursue a degree in Information Technology Management and Cybersecurity. I wanted to better understand the technology I was already interested in and develop the skills needed to turn that interest into a career. Throughout my degree, I have studied networking, cybersecurity, programming, databases, data analytics, and web development, which has allowed me to experience several different areas of the IT field. As I progressed through the program, I became especially interested in how networks and systems are built, managed, and secured. My coursework has also included hands-on labs and projects that have given me the opportunity to apply what I learn instead of only studying the concepts behind it. These experiences have helped shape my interests within the field and have given me a clearer idea of where I want to take my career after graduation.

As I continued developing my skills outside of the classroom, I earned my CompTIA Network+ certification and began building projects that challenged me to put what I had learned into practice. I have worked with technologies such as Python, Linux, Git, virtual machines, and HTML while building projects for both my coursework and personal portfolio. Working through these projects has taught me more than just how to use different tools. I have learned how to troubleshoot problems, research solutions when I get stuck, and understand why something works instead of simply trying to get it working. I enjoy that part of technology because there is always something new to learn and another problem to figure out.

After graduation, my goal is to start my career in information technology or cybersecurity where I can continue learning from real world experience and build on the foundation I have developed through school and my own projects. I am interested in opportunities that will allow me to strengthen my skills in networking, systems, and security. As I gain experience, I would eventually like to move into cloud security and work with the technologies used to secure modern cloud environments. I know there is still a lot for me to learn, and that is one of the things that motivates me about starting a career in this field.

My Personal Interests and hobbies

A lot of my drive to keep improving comes from my time in the U.S. Marine Corps. Serving in the infantry taught me to become comfortable with being challenged, take control of my own development, and constantly look for ways to improve. I carried that mindset throughout my service and progressed to the rank of Sergeant within roughly three years, taking on leadership responsibilities early in my career. That experience showed me what I am capable of when I commit myself to learning, improving, and taking on greater responsibility. I have carried that same mindset with me into technology. I am rarely satisfied with simply knowing enough to complete something; I want to understand how it works, why it works, and what I can do with it. There is always a feeling that I could be doing more, learning something new, or challenging myself in a different way. That is one of the reasons technology has been such a good fit for me. The field never really gives you an opportunity to stop learning. Whether I am working through a technical problem, building a new project, or learning a technology I have never used before, I enjoy starting with something unfamiliar and gradually reaching the point where I understand it.

Outside of technology, I enjoy spending time with my family and playing and watching basketball. I try to bring the same sense of discipline and continuous improvement into the things I pursue personally and professionally. For me, progress is one of the most rewarding parts of any challenge, and I am always looking for the next opportunity to learn, improve, and take on something more difficult.
